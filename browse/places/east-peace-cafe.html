Peace Middle East Cafe

★★★★☆ (4.5/5.0)

A hidden gem in Richardson offering a cozy, tranquil escape. The place is renowned for its authentic Arabic coffee, creative lattes, and decadent Middle Eastern desserts, perfect for focused studying or relaxing with friends.

📍 581 W Campbell Rd Unit #125, Richardson, TX 75080, United States
🤫 Noise Level: Quiet to Moderate
📶 WiFi: Good (60 Mbps)

About This Space

Peace Middle East Cafe is a sanctuary in Richardson filled with traditional culinary and a cozy, welcoming atmosphere. This Middle Eastern style cafe provides a peaceful retreat ideal for both solo study sessions and late-night outings with friends. They open until very late, making it convenient for college students who have to stay up the night to cram for projects or tests. The décor is thoughtful and inviting, enhanced by soft ambient music that encourages relaxation and focus. The staff are known for being super welcoming, helpful, and attentive, contributing to the cafe's reputation as a relaxing late-night study spot. I remember getting two free desserts from the owner, who was also so welcoming. Makes you want to visit often!

The cafe stands out for its incredible menu that goes far beyond standard coffee shop fare. They are renowned for their rich, aromatic Arabic coffee and innovative specialty drinks like the Saffron Matcha Latte. The food is a highlight, with a vast selection of made-to-order sweet and savory crepes, gourmet waffles, authentic desserts like Turkish Baklava and Baklava Cheesecake, and even savory pies and toasts. Everything is beautifully presented and crafted with care, with customers noting the homemade taste and full flavor.

Study Tips for This Location

  • An excellent spot for late-night studying, as they are open until 2:00 AM.
  • Try their signature drinks like the Saffron Matcha Latte or traditional Arabic coffee on sand for a unique experience.
  • The chocolate pistachio crepe and baklava cheesecake are highly recommended for a study treat.
  • The ambiance is cozy and conversation-friendly; it's quiet but not a silent library.
  • As a family establishment where food is made to order, please be patient during busy times.

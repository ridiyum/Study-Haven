About This Space

The Eugene McDermott Library is UT Dallas's main academic library, offering a premier study environment for students across all disciplines. With multiple floors dedicated to different study styles, you can choose between completely silent zones on upper floors, quiet study areas, or collaborative spaces on the lower levels. The library features modern amenities, comfortable furniture, and an atmosphere that promotes deep focus and academic achievement.

Resources include extensive book collections, academic journals, Special Collections, computer labs with specialized software, private study rooms (bookable in advance through the library website), and comprehensive printing services. The library staff is knowledgeable and helpful, ready to assist with research questions, citation help, or technical issues. This is the go-to location for UTD students during exam season or when working on major projects and research papers.

Study Tips for This Location

  • Book study rooms online through the library website - reserve in advance during exam weeks
  • 4th floor is the silent floor - strictly enforced quiet policy, no phone calls or group discussions
  • Lower floors allow for collaborative work and group discussions
  • Bring your Comet Card for building access and printing
  • The library is open 24/7 during finals week
  • Pack snacks and water - vending machines on first floor only
  • Use the online catalog and databases from anywhere with your UTD login

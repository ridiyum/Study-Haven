Fukuro

★★★★☆ (4.5/5.0)

Modern Asian-inspired cafe with tech-friendly atmosphere and strong WiFi. Perfect blend of style and functionality for digital workers.

📍 3000 Northside Blvd Suite 600, Richardson, TX 75080, United States
🤫 Noise Level: Moderate-Quiet
📶 WiFi: Excellent (80 Mbps)

About This Space

Fukuro is a contemporary cafe that perfectly balances aesthetic appeal with practical functionality. The space features modern Asian-inspired decor with clean lines, warm lighting, and thoughtfully designed seating areas. What sets Fukuro apart is its tech-friendly approach - every seat has easy access to power outlets, and the WiFi is consistently fast and reliable.

The cafe serves a variety of specialty drinks including matcha lattes, fruit teas, and creative coffee beverages, along with light snacks and desserts. The atmosphere is welcoming to students and remote workers, with a nice balance between social energy and focused productivity. The staff is friendly and understanding of customers who stay for extended periods.

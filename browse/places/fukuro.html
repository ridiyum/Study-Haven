Fukuro

★★★★☆ (4.5/5.0)

A fresh and vibrant spot near the UTD campus, popular for customizable poke bowls and creative bubble tea. A cozy place for casual studying or a meal break with friends.

📍 3000 Northside Blvd Suite 600, Richardson, TX 75080, United States
🤫 Noise Level: Moderate
📶 WiFi: Free (See Note)

About This Space

Fukuro is that go-to spot right near UTD campus when you need fresh, quality food and maybe want to knock out some light studying at the same time. It's known for customizable poke bowls, solid sushi and ramen, and bubble tea that actually hits. The vibe is cozy and laid-back with a tech-friendly atmosphere—you'll see plenty of students with laptops out, coding between bites or reviewing notes over boba. People come here to hang out, grab a meal with friends, or squeeze in some homework between classes. It's definitely more of a restaurant than a dedicated study cafe, but that's part of the appeal.

The menu is all about freshness and letting you build what you want. Their poke bowls are made to order with quality fish and veggies, and their boba drinks (especially the milk green teas) are a crowd favorite. There's free WiFi that's reliable enough for most work, and the atmosphere is relaxed enough that you won't feel weird pulling out your laptop or tablet for a bit. Just keep in mind that seating is limited, so this works best for casual work sessions or meal breaks rather than marathon study grinds. Plus, there's ample parking right outside, which is clutch when you're in a rush.

Study Tips for This Location

  • Remember, this is a restaurant first—perfect for combining a meal with light studying, not all-day cramming sessions.
  • Seating fills up fast during lunch and dinner rushes, so off-peak afternoon hours are your best bet.
  • Take advantage of the free WiFi and easy parking situation—great for quick work sessions between classes.
  • They have paper order sheets you can fill out, which is convenient if you want to order quickly without interrupting your work flow.
  • Great spot for group projects where you want to discuss over food in a casual, tech-friendly setting.
